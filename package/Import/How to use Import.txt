IMPORT - drop games and music here
==================================
Copy games, music and BIOS files into this folder from your PC. The next time
Clove opens, it moves each one into the right console folder in Roms.

* Most games are recognised by their file type: .gba .gbc .gb .nes .sfc
  .smc .md .sms .gg .pce .ngp .ws .lnx .a26 and many more.
* PlayStation discs (.cue + .bin, .chd, .pbp, .m3u) are recognised too.
* Arcade .zip files are recognised by their MAME name.
* BIOS files (scph1001.bin, gba_bios.bin, disksys.rom, neogeo.zip ...)
  go into the BIOS folder.
* Music (.mp3 .flac .ogg .wav .m4a .aac .opus ...) goes into the Music
  folder - single songs or whole album folders - and plays from the
  Music tab. Music videos (.mp4 with a picture) go to Videos.
* Not sure it'll be recognised? Make a folder in here named after the
  console (GBA, SNES, PS1, Mega Drive, Arcade ...) and put it inside.

Anything Clove can't place stays here and appears as "Import" in the
Consoles list on the handheld, where you can choose the console.
